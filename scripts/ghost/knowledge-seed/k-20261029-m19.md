`container_system.py` runs the cycle count given at launch (`-c <cycles>`) and exits normally without resetting BOP controls. Afterwards `roots_blower_enable`, `extraction_vacuum_enable`, pumps etc. stay at their last commanded state, `vent_or_bag_valve` stays where it was, the accumulator may drain to ~0 psi over hours, and supervisor lock stays 0.

`vent_or_bag_valve`: `0 = vent` (CO2 to atmosphere, not feeding the line); `1 = bag` (bag → compressor → BOP accumulator → liquefaction; normal production).

From telemetry alone this looks like a fault. Check automation console for the script finishing first. Signs: healthy accumulator earlier, gradual drain over hours after a long extraction run, no abrupt state changes.

2026-07-22: accumulator at −3 psi for hours was treated as a possible fault; the script had simply finished its cycles.
