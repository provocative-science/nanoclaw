At 05:42:20 UTC on 2026-10-29 the panel station (192.168.105.100) stopped answering: ~45 panel fields went null together, including `vent_or_bag_valve`. `extraction_bag_watchdog` trips when its inputs are missing (not only on bag angle out of range), so it locked at 05:42:22 and started `extraction_bag_recovery`, whose writes failed with `Modbus(Device(MemoryParityError))` every ~4.5 s until the station came back at 07:07:03 UTC. The lock stayed until it was cleared at 12:31 UTC. Why the station dropped is not established.

The pin remap `731e0e8` on the same riser was live but is not the cause: its output was not written for 8.7h before onset, and a remap changes which coil one output drives; it cannot make a whole station stop answering. The earlier watchdog trips at 19:33 and 20:09 UTC on Oct 28 recovered normally.

How to check next time: `mcp__telemetry__why_locked(host="airbed-host", at=...)` gives the null fields by station, the watchdog's stale inputs and a change check with a mechanism test.
