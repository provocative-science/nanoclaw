2026-10-29 05:42:22 UTC airbed lock. `vent_or_bag_valve` and ~45 panel-station fields went null at 05:42:20 together (panel backplane Modbus). `extraction_bag_watchdog` trips when its inputs are missing (not only on bag angle out of 6.5–9.9), so it fired and started `extraction_bag_recovery`, which then failed repeatedly with `Modbus(Device(MemoryParityError))`. Bag angle stayed 10.0 and the compressor was off.

How to check next time: `mcp__telemetry__why_locked(host="airbed-host", at=...)` lists fields that went null at the edge grouped by station, the watchdog named in console, and whether its inputs were stale. Many nulls at once = device/IO fault first, process cause second.
