A Planet IMG-2100T Modbus TCP→RTU bridge is still in the liquefaction control cabinet; the only device on that RTU bus is the Alicat flow controller. The same bridge model was removed from BoP/DAC (2026-05-20) for answering packets addressed to other devices with wrong MAC addresses. Suspect it for dropped or erroneous Alicat readings/connections.
