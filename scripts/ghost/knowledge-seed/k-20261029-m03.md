Disallow (and never advise) enabling liquid valves without the TC probe heaters on.

Why (2026-05-18, ~16 lb liquid CO2 lost): ~21:22 UTC the control panel power tripped; the system came back with TC probe heaters off. Unheated TC probes falsely read "liquid present", the liquid valves enabled and drained the holding tank; O2 spiked to 1000 ppm at 21:30 UTC as the tank vented. Root cause: no interlock preventing liquid valves from enabling without TC probe heaters active (interlock still to be implemented as of the note).
