Evidence (2026-10-01 investigation):
- Loki control-console showed a crash/restart loop ~2026-09-14 20:05–20:46 ET with Modbus warnings on `Bop(Interface(Modbus(Device(Custom(33)))))` (`HeaderMismatch`, `ExceptionResponse MemoryParityError`) and an `extraction_bag_interlock` block ("cannot set enable roots blower while extraction pressure 101.094406 is above 40").
- That 101.09 psi `extraction_vacuum_gas_in_pressure` was flat at the same value for 58+ hours before (back to ~Sep 11): a long-standing stale read on a flaky Modbus device, not a new event.
- Loki went silent after 20:46 ET while metrics continued ~8 h (log tailing broke, not the process).
- The last good scrape (2026-09-15 04:36 ET) showed no degradation: ticktime steady ~300 ms, supervisor lock 0, all pump/blower power 0 (idle).

Inference (not confirmed, nobody physically checked): abrupt host/network failure (power, crash or network), not a graceful shutdown or supervisor fault.

Related: during 2026-09-17 18:00–09-18 04:00 ET the BOP kept running and feeding (roots blower and extraction vacuum enabled, no lock, product flow nonzero), so the liquefaction accumulator plateau at ~18:30 ET that day was liquefaction-side.
