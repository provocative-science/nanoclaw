`date_trunc` / `to_timestamp` on the TIMESTAMPTZ `timestamp` column throws a `pytz` import error in this DuckDB. Bucket with `floor(epoch(timestamp)/N)*N` instead. Use `query_sql` with an explicit `host=` (do not rely on defaults).
